The Platform

One foundation. Your entire gaming ecosystem.

Modern PC gaming is fragmented across stores, launchers, executables, configurations, mods and services. VoidOne is designed as a native platform layer between you, your games and your operating system.

🎮

Game Library

Build a unified view of the games already installed or owned across your gaming ecosystem.

👻

Ghost Launcher

A planned controlled execution layer for game launching, arguments, process management and runtime state.

🧰

Mod Platform

A future modular environment for profiles, dependencies, conflicts and non-destructive game configuration.

🩺

Diagnostics

Understand application and game failures through structured diagnostics, logs and runtime information.

📊

Local Analytics

Useful gaming and performance insights designed around local ownership rather than behavioral tracking.

🔌

Extensions

A long-term developer ecosystem for providers, themes, integrations and community extensions.

VoidOne Intelligence

Your AI. Your choice.

VoidOne is not designed around forcing users into a single proprietary AI. The long-term architecture is provider independent — allowing local models, cloud providers or no AI at all.

Local

Run it yourself.

Connect local models, runtimes and hardware acceleration while keeping inference under your control.

Cloud

Bring your provider.

Use a cloud provider when local inference is not practical, without coupling the entire platform to one AI.

Gateway

One integration layer.

The planned AI Gateway abstracts providers from the rest of VoidOne, keeping the architecture replaceable.

Philosophy

Engineering over hype.

VoidOne is designed around a simple idea: software should give the user more control, not quietly take it away.

🔒 Privacy by Design

Your information should not be collected, transmitted or monetized without a legitimate technical reason.

💾 Local First

Important gaming data should remain under your control whenever technically practical.

⚡ Lightweight by Design

Dependencies and background services should justify their resource cost.

🌐 Open by Design

VoidOne is built to remain inspectable, modifiable, extensible and accessible to contributors.

🧠 User-Controlled Intelligence

Users decide whether AI is used, which AI is used and what it can access.

📐 Evidence Over Marketing

Technical claims should be supported by implementation, testing, benchmarks and reproducible evidence.
